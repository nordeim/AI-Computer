Most people associate wool with cold weather. We saw an opportunity to re-engineer nature's original performance fabric for the world's most demanding urban climate.

The Philosophy

Merino wool is not what you think. Each fiber is one-tenth the diameter of a human hair — creating a material that breathes in heat, insulates in air-conditioning, wicks moisture faster than synthetics, and resists odour naturally. We didn't invent wool. We just asked it to do more.

Born in New Zealand highlands. Tested on Singapore's 32°C streets. Every pair is climate-certified for tropical urban life — from MRT platforms to hawker centre queues.
