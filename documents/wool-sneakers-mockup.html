Our Philosophy

From Alpine Meadows
to Singapore Streets

We believed merino wool could do more than keep you warm. So we re-engineered it for the world's most demanding urban climate.

Natural Merino

Sourced from New Zealand highland farms. Each fiber is 1/10th the diameter of human hair — creating a naturally breathable, temperature-regulating material.

Urban Engineered

Designed for concrete, not countryside. Reinforced outsoles for MRT grilles, cushioned insoles for hawker centre queues, and ventilation for 90% humidity days.

Circular by Design

Merino is biodegradable and renewable. Our packaging uses zero plastic. Every pair comes with a return programme — when you're done, we compost them.
